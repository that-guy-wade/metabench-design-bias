13 of 36 instructed sessions were labelled PARTIAL_AMBIGUOUS: Wonder 5 of 18, Spark 1.3 contributor 8 of 18.

**What they did.** All 13 follow the same "try, then refuse" pattern.
- In early rounds the designer said it would look for items favouring the target and probed slices where the target
